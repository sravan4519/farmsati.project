# 🌱 FarmSathi – AI Urban Farming Assistant

> **Healthier Plants. Smarter Care.**

FarmSathi is an AI-powered Urban Farming Assistant designed to help urban gardeners and plant growers understand their plants, identify possible health problems, and make better plant-care decisions.

## 🚀 Live Demo

🔗 https://farmsatiwebsite.ai.studio

## 🎯 Problem

Urban gardeners often struggle with:

- Identifying plant diseases and health problems
- Knowing when and how much to water
- Understanding the effect of weather on plants
- Maintaining plant-care routines
- Finding reliable agricultural information

FarmSathi brings these needs together into one simple platform.

## 💡 Solution

FarmSathi combines:

- 🌿 Plant health monitoring
- 🤖 AI-powered plant assistance
- 📷 Leaf image analysis
- 💧 Smart watering recommendations
- 🌦️ Weather-based plant care
- 📊 Plant tracking and health monitoring
- 🌱 Growth and care guidance
- 🧑‍🌾 Agricultural resources and government schemes

## ✨ Key Features

### 🌿 AI Plant Doctor
Users can upload a plant or leaf image and receive possible causes and basic care recommendations.

### 💧 Smart Watering
Watering recommendations consider the plant type and weather conditions instead of relying only on a fixed schedule.

### 🌦️ Weather Integration
FarmSathi uses weather information to provide more relevant plant-care recommendations.

### 🌱 My Plants
Users can add and track their plants, including their age, health, watering and care information.

### 🤖 AI Assistant
Users can ask natural-language questions about their plants and receive practical guidance.

### 🏛️ Government Schemes
The platform provides agricultural scheme and subsidy information to help users discover relevant resources.

## 🧠 AI Concept

FarmSathi combines plant information, user-provided symptoms, weather conditions and care history to provide personalized plant-care guidance.

The goal is not just to provide information, but to help the user understand:

> **"What does my plant need, and what should I do next?"**
